04 / Under the hood

Linux-native by design.

No extra desktop shell, Electron runtime, or always-on service.

Requirements

GlassDock is designed for a Wayland session with wlr-layer-shell. Python 3.9+, GTK 3, PyGObject, Pycairo, and gtk-layer-shell are required. Sway or SwayFX is recommended for running-window information.

Transparency depends on your compositor. Background blur specifically requires SwayFX and its layer_effects support.
